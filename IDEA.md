# Idea Factory — brief

## One-liner
Ecosystem-intelligence engine for an entrepreneur network: continuously ingests startup/market signals, runs them through AI analysts wearing chosen personas, and surfaces actionable new-venture ideas — especially cross-market arbitrage (e.g. adapt a US product to Turkey, or improve a product launched in another market).

## Who
- An existing network of entrepreneurs (founder + friends) who regularly brainstorm and build new ventures together.
- Internal tool first; possible product for other founder networks later.

## Core pain
- Impossible to stay on top of the full startup literature + keep up with new developments solo.
- New + growing startups, funding/investment news scattered across many sources.
- Brainstorming is unstructured; no systematic pipeline from "signal" → "analyzed idea".

## What it does
1. **Ingest** data from many sources:
   - Y Combinator (launches, companies)
   - TLDR-style newsletters / aggregator sites
   - Startup influencers
   - LinkedIn, Twitter/X
   - "a bunch of websites" — extensible source list
2. **Track**: new startups, growing startups, funding rounds, investment news.
3. **Analyze with AI personas**: assign the AI a persona / point-of-view, and have it analyze the pulled data through that lens.
4. **Generate ideas**: focus on sector/market shifts —
   - Adapt a US/foreign product to Turkey
   - Take a product launched in another market and improve it
   - Cross-market arbitrage in general
5. **Inform**: provide the network broad, ongoing ecosystem intelligence.

## Vision
A true "idea factory": signal in → analysis + ranked venture ideas out, continuously.
